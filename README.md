## 中文

**沉砾（Sedgrit）**——一款"向内生长"的湖面作品。整个界面是一幅晨光中的湖光山色：天空、森林、远山、木屋、石头垒起的湖岸、清透的青绿湖水。你做的每一件"不逃"的小事，都会变成一块石头，从空中落入湖心。

**这不是游戏。** 无奖励、无成就、无红点、无社交。可以不打开，石头仍在；几天不来，只是沉积得更深。

### 三种"不逃"

| 动作 | 落石 |
|---|---|
| ✎ 写一句真实 | 板岩/棱角石：字数 → 体积；直面负面情绪 → 更沉 |
| ◷ 静坐 | 沉默之石：坐满 1 分钟起成石，中途退出只得半块 |
| ● 每日到场 | 月晕微光石：连续天数 → 沉积加成 |

### 玩法

- **落石**：石头从天空坠入湖心，泛起三环涟漪与水下金光，然后沉入看不见的深处
- **星光**：每块石头在水下亮起一颗金色十字星，积累看得见（最多 40 颗）
- **里程碑**：第 3 / 7 / 14 块时湖面泛起金光提示
- **基岩仪式**：攒够 20 块，湖水退去，露出它们压成的基岩，基岩上立起一株树——树随真实天数缓慢生长、随风轻摇
- **互动**：点湖面起涟漪、点天空惊起飞鸟、静坐时画面与湖声渐静
- **湖声**：真实录音（水波 + 鸟鸣），首次点击页面后响起

### 隐私与伦理

- 数据只存本机（localStorage），不上传、不联网、零追踪；单文件 HTML，可离线使用
- 写入文字若触发心理危机词，会弹出援助热线（全国 12356 / 北京 010-82951332 / 120）
- 本工具是自我觉察的辅助载体，不替代心理咨询或治疗

## English

**Sedgrit** — a quiet, inward-growing lake. The whole interface is a morning lakeside painting: sky, forest, distant mountains, a wooden cabin, a stone-piled shore, and clear teal water. Every small act of not running away — writing one true sentence, sitting still, showing up today — drops a stone into the lake.

**This is not a game.** No rewards, no achievements, no badges, no social. You may stay away — the stones remain, settling deeper.

### Three ways to drop a stone

| Action | Stone |
|---|---|
| ✎ Write one true sentence | Slate/jagged stone — length sets size; facing dark feelings makes it denser |
| ◷ Sit still | Stone of silence — 1 minute or more; leaving early yields only half |
| ● Show up today | Moon-glow stone — streaks give a settling bonus |

### Gameplay

- **Falling stone** — a stone drops from the sky into the lake, ripples and a golden glow spread, then it sinks out of sight
- **Star lights** — each stone lights a golden cross-star underwater; you can see your accumulation (up to 40)
- **Milestones** — golden ripples at the 3rd / 7th / 14th stone
- **The ritual** — at 20 stones the water recedes, revealing the bedrock they have formed; a tree grows from the bedrock — taller each real day, swaying in the wind
- **Interactions** — click the lake for ripples, click the sky to startle birds; the scene and lake sounds quiet down while you sit
- **Lake sounds** — real recordings (water + birds), unlocked on your first click

### Privacy & Ethics

- All data stays on this device (localStorage). No uploads, no network, no tracking. Single-file HTML, works offline
- If your writing triggers a crisis signal, hotlines appear (National 12356 / Beijing 010-82951332 / 120)
- This tool is a companion for self-awareness, not a substitute for professional counseling or treatment
